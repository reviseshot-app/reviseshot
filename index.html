Where do I find my Machine Code?

It appears in the activation window when you open the app. The "Buy" button fills it in for you, or click "Copy" and paste it into the payment form.

Yes. Windows shows this for new apps from small developers. Click More info and then Run anyway.

No. One license is for one PC. Another PC needs its own license.

What if I reinstall Windows?

How soon will I get my key?

Usually within a few hours (orders after 10 PM are handled the next morning).
